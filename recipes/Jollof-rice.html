Jollof-rice

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish known for its rich flavor, vibrant red color, and irresistible aroma. It is made by cooking rice in a savory tomato-based sauce blended with peppers, onions, and spices, which allows the grains to absorb deep, smoky flavors as they simmer.

Often served at parties, celebrations, and family gatherings, jollof rice is commonly paired with fried plantains, chicken, beef, or fish. Its bold taste and cultural significance make it more than just a meal—it is a symbol of togetherness and pride across West Africa.

Ingredient

  • rice
  • meat
  • tomatoes
  • peppers
  • onions
  • garlic
  • ginger
  • curry powder
  • bay leaves
  • thyme
  • bouillon/stock

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and set it aside
  2. Blend tomatoes, red bell peppers, onions, and scotch bonnet (pepper)
  3. Heat oil in a pot and sauté chopped onions until fragrant
  4. Add tomato paste and fry for a few minutes to remove sour taste
  5. Pour in the blended tomato mixture and cook until the sauce thickens and oil rises
  6. Season with salt, seasoning cubes, curry, thyme, and bay leaf (optional)
  7. Add stock or water and bring to a boil
  8. Add the washed rice and stir well
  9. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice absorbs the sauce and becomes tender
  10. Stir gently, adjust seasoning if needed, and allow to steam for a few minutes
  11. Serve hot with chicken, fish, salad, or fried plantain
