Vegetable-stir-fly Recipes

Vegetable Stir-Fry is a quick, colorful, and healthy dish made by cooking a mix of fresh vegetables over high heat. It is popular because it preserves the natural crunch, flavor, and nutrients of the vegetables while bringing them together with a light, savory sauce. This recipe is flexible, allowing you to use whatever vegetables are available in your kitchen.

The dish is perfect as a main meal or a side dish and can be served with rice, noodles, or flatbread. With minimal oil and fast cooking time, vegetable stir-fry is ideal for busy days when you want something nutritious, tasty, and satisfying.

Ingredient

  • Mixed vegetables (carrots, bell peppers, cabbage, green beans, broccoli)
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Fresh Ginger
  • Vegetable oil
  • Soy sauce
  • Black pepper
  • salt
  • Seasoning cube (optional)
  • Sesame oil (optional)

Steps

  1. Wash all vegetables thoroughly and cut them into bite-size pieces
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a pan or wok over medium-high heat.
  3. Add sliced onions, garlic, and ginger; stir for a few seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the vegetables and stir continuously for 3–5 minutes.
  5. Add soy sauce, salt, pepper, and seasoning cube if using; mix well.
  6. Stir-fry until vegetables are tender but still slightly crunchy.
  7. Drizzle a little sesame oil (optional), stir once more, and remove from heat.
